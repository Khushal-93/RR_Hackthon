1. PRESSURE DEFINITION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Pressure is defined strictly as the TOP THIRD of Leverage Index standardized within each innings:
  - Innings 1: Leverage Index standardized across all Innings 1 legal deliveries.
    High Pressure = Percentile Rank >= 66.7% (Leverage >= 1.258).
    75.54% of Innings 1 High Pressure falls in Overs 1-6 (Powerplay) and 86.99% is faced by Orders 1-3.
    It captures Early Foundation Volatility (surviving swing while setting target).
  - Innings 2: Leverage Index standardized across all Innings 2 legal deliveries.
    High Pressure = Percentile Rank >= 66.7% (Leverage >= 1.246).
    Distributed across middle and death overs under escalating Required Run Rate.
    It captures Target-Chasing Execution under match-outcome volatility.

2. METRICS & BASELINES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Score Specification (Pure Run Efficiency):
   - Batters: Shrunk opposition-adjusted Runs Above Expected (RAE) per high-pressure ball.
     RAE_i,t = runs_t - (E_{-m}[runs | phase, WIH, batting_order, bowler_type] + Delta_bowl_team / 6)
   - Bowlers: Shrunk opposition-adjusted Runs Saved (RSE) per high-pressure ball.
     RSE_i,t = (E_{-m}[runs | phase, WIH, batting_order] + Delta_bat_team / 6) - total_delivery_runs_t
   - Dismissal Rate vs Expected: Reported as an independent diagnostic column only.
     Out-of-time validation proved that adding binary wicket outcomes (k*WAE) degrades cross-era
     repeatability (rho drops from +0.23 to -0.004 for batters, and from +0.31 to +0.10 for bowlers).
     Therefore, wickets are excluded from the score to maximize signal-to-noise.

B. Empirical Bayes Shrinkage Formulation:
   Each player's observed sample mean y_i is shrunk toward the pool grand mean mu0:
     y_shrunk,i = lambda_i * y_i + (1 - lambda_i) * mu0
     lambda_i   = tau^2 / (tau^2 + v_i)
     v_i        = sigma_i^2 / N_i  (true delivery-level sampling variance)
   - Batters Pool: mu0 = -0.0361 runs/b, tau^2 = 0.0182, mean(v_i) = 0.0098
   - Bowlers Pool: mu0 = +0.0788 runs/b, tau^2 = 0.0112, mean(v_i) = 0.0103

3. FAIRNESS: SAMPLE SIZE, ELIGIBILITY, IPL VS T20I, AND DLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Strict Non-DLS Sample: 100% of rain-shortened and DLS-affected matches (41 matches, 5,254 deliveries)
     were excluded from model baselines and player ratings. A test enforces 0 DLS deliveries.
  2. Eligibility Threshold: Players must face >= 150 high-pressure legal balls and play >= 15 matches.
     This eliminates small-sample outliers while retaining 118 eligible batters and 124 eligible bowlers.
  3. Empirical Bayes Equalizer: Players with moderate volume (150-250 balls) are safely shrunk
     toward mu0 (lambda ~ 0.45-0.55), preventing small-sample spikes from dominating high-volume veterans.
  4. Competition & Opposition Quality: Expected baseline runs are adjusted for opponent bowling/batting
     attack strength (shrunk toward global economy with m=300 prior). Batters facing international
     bowling attacks receive upward credit (+0.05 to +0.08 runs/ball), balancing higher IPL venue baselines.

     Top-10 Overlap = 3/10 (Chance: 1.59/10) | Top-20% Overlap = 25.0% (Chance: 20.0%)

B. Plain Limitations Assessment:
   1. Signal is Modest: Across disjoint seasons, rank correlation is rho ~ 0.25 - 0.29, with top-20%
      overlap around 25-30% (better than chance 20%, but far from deterministic). These ratings describe
      historical efficiency in high-pressure corridors, NOT high-precision predictive traits.
   2. Wickets Reduced Repeatability: Testing candidates confirmed that adding k*WAE to rate metrics
      destroys out-of-time stability due to Bernoulli dismissal variance. Bowler ranking ordering
      in particular is heavily clustered with statistical ties (~50% pairwise probabilities between adjacent
      ranks) and must be understood as confidence tiers rather than discrete rank numbers.
   3. Middle-Overs Spin Tactical Confounding: The top-10 bowler list is 60.0% spin (vs 33.6% in the pool),
      and 5 of those spinners bowl 63.5% to 91.8% of their balls in Middle overs (overs 7-15). While state
      baselines adjust for phase and wickets in hand, middle overs provide structurally different run-suppression
      dynamics compared to powerplay and death pace corridors. Role-stratified evaluations are recommended.
   4. Low-Volume Sensitivity near Floor: For qualifiers near 150-220 balls, bowler rankings show sensitivity
      to peak matches: dropping the top 2 matches moves Behrendorff, Pathirana, and Motie from top 10 into
      ranks #28-#32, while Muqeem (#9) and batters David (#13) and Klaasen (#17) maintain top-tier stability.
   5. No Psychological Attribution: Models measure delivered runs and wickets in historical states.
      They do not quantify mental toughness, choking, or tactical intent.
