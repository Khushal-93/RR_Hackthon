Key Empirical Findings

1. Signal is Modest, Not Deterministic
Out-of-time correlation across disjoint seasons (2022-2024 → 2025-2026) is ρ ≈ +0.25 to +0.29. Ratings describe historical efficiency, not fixed personality traits.
2. Pure Run Efficiency Trumps Wickets
Testing candidate formulas proved that injecting binary dismissals into ratings destroys out-of-sample repeatability. Wickets are reported purely as diagnostic columns.
3. Innings-1 Pressure is Early Powerplay
75.5% of Innings 1 pressure falls in overs 1–6 as top-order batters face early swing and target-setting volatility. Chasing pressure escalates in overs 16–20.

Methodological Integrity & Scope Limits

100% of rain-shortened and DLS-affected matches were excluded to prevent contaminated resources. Ratings near the 150-ball floor are sensitive to peak matches. Results represent situational rate efficiency, not psychological bravery or "clutch" character.
