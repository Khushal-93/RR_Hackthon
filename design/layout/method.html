Honest Scientific Assessment:
Cross-era rank correlation is modest (ρ ≈ +0.25 to +0.29). For batters, the 95% bootstrap confidence interval [-0.071, +0.498] spans zero, indicating that batter pressure efficiency is not statistically distinguishable from random variation across independent multi-year eras. For bowlers, the CI [+0.010, +0.502] strictly excludes zero. These ratings describe historical situational efficiency, NOT permanent predictive traits.

METHODOLOGICAL CEMETERY: WHAT WE TESTED AND DROPPED

1. Raw Win Probability Added (WPA)
Why Dropped: Plain cumulative WPA showed near-zero cross-period stability (ρ = 0.000 to 0.045) and rewarded players simply for being on the field during high-volatility finishes. It measured situational opportunity rather than player quality.
2. Combined Score with Wickets (RAE + k×WAE)
Why Dropped: Dismissals occur on only ~5% of deliveries. Multiplying this binary event by runs-per-wicket (k ≈ 25) injected massive Bernoulli noise, collapsing batter out-of-time repeatability from ρ = +0.23 to -0.004.
3. Flat Run-per-Wicket Weight (k = 20 or 25)
Why Dropped: Wicket value changes by a factor of 4× across the match (from ~35 runs lost in the Powerplay to ~8 runs in the 20th over). Even dynamic state-dependent wicket loss degraded cross-era signal compared to pure run efficiency.
4. Global (Unstandardized) Leverage Terciles
Why Dropped: A single global threshold caused Innings 2 death overs to crowd out all Innings 1 deliveries, falsely assuming that early target-setting carries negligible pressure. Within-innings standardization restored fairness.
Documented Limitations & Boundary Conditions
1. Middle-Overs Spin Tactical Confounding: 60% of top-10 bowlers are spinners (vs 33.6% in the pool), and 5 bowl predominantly in Middle overs (overs 7–15). While state baselines control for phase and wickets in hand, middle overs provide structurally favorable run suppression compared to boundary-hunting death overs.
2. Fragility Near the Eligibility Floor: For players near 150–220 balls, removing 2 peak matches causes bowler ranks to drop 20+ positions into the #28–#32 range. Ranks near the floor are confidence bands, not definitive ordinal spots.
3. Strict Exclusion of Corrupted Matches: 41 rain-shortened and DLS-affected matches (5,254 balls) and 38 super-over balls were excluded from all models. Scorecards with RRR error within ±10 runs were flagged approximate.
4. Descriptive Historical Nature: The model evaluates what players delivered in historical states relative to expectation. It does not measure psychological bravery, choking, or future predictive traits.
