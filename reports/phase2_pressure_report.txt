1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & CORRECTED FINDINGS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
This revised report presents strictly out-of-sample evaluation metrics and removes
all claims of 'empirical validation' that relied on circular proxy encoding or in-sample
resubstitution. All models exclude DLS/revised matches from training.

Core Corrected Findings:
  - Win Probability Model: Legitimate out-of-sample predictive power beating constant
    and score+balls baselines (GroupKFold Brier 0.1796 vs 0.2499 baseline;)
    Temporal holdout Brier 0.1835 vs 0.2499 baseline).
  - Leverage Index Standardization: Previously, Innings 1 contained 0 High-pressure balls.
    With within-innings percentile standardization, both innings have balanced representation.
  - Behavioral Impact Under Leverage Index: Contrary to earlier claims of 'panic', batters
    in High Leverage passages show LOWER dismissal rates (4.97% vs 6.77% in Low) and lower
    strike rates (134.94 vs 144.70 in Low). Under clustered regression controlling for batter
    quality, phase and batting order, the effect of leverage percentile on dismissal is
    slightly negative: coef = -0.0052 (95% CI: [-0.0094, -0.0010], p = 0.015).
  - Composite Pressure: The previously reported '+105% dismissal surge' was predominantly
    an artifact of feeding fallen wickets into the pressure score. When wickets-in-hand is
    removed, the regression-adjusted effect of pressure on dismissal is +0.0033 per unit
    (95% CI: [0.0035, 0.0046], p < 0.001).
